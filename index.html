About me

I'm a Business Information Systems undergraduate at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, with a strong interest in project management and business analysis. Throughout my university journey, I've worked at the intersection of people, processes, and technology — coordinating teams, managing initiatives, organizing large-scale events, and translating ideas and stakeholder needs into structured, actionable plans.

As the President of the ISACA Student Group, I lead chapter initiatives from planning and coordination to execution, while working closely with members, stakeholders, and external partners. Alongside my leadership experience, I've managed academic projects using tools such as ClickUp and Notion, developed hands-on experience with MySQL and other technologies, and gained practical exposure to banking operations through my internship at People's Bank.

I'm now looking to build a career in business analysis or project management, where I can combine analytical thinking, stakeholder collaboration, and project coordination to turn complex requirements into practical solutions and successfully deliver projects.
